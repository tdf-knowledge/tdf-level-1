# TDF level-1 register: what is already public

**What this is.** Every row below is a fact that anyone can already read on a public page, with the page that shows it. Level 1 (L1) means public anywhere. Any L1 fact may also appear at every higher level.

**What the source-check label means.** "public-source check recorded 29 Oct 2026; unverified" means someone opened the linked public page on that date and saw the claim stated there. It does **not** mean TDF has reviewed it, and it does not mean the claim is true: a TDF website or blog says what TDF says. Where public pages disagree, see Conflicts. A named TDF reviewer can later record a stronger status through a pull request.

**How to read it**
- **The source decides the kind of claim.** `W-` and `B-` sources are TDF's own website and blog: the fact is what TDF *says*, not an independent check. Press and directory sources (`P-`, `D-`) repeat or report.
- **"search snippet only"** in the Status column means a search-result summary showed the fact but the page itself was not opened.
- **Numbers change over time.** Where sources disagree, the row says so and the disagreement is also listed in [Conflicts](#conflicts-between-public-sources).
- **What is left out on purpose.** Public pages also hold personal details about named people, such as health, family and private money. Those are not listed here. Being public does not make them suitable for TDF's knowledge base, so they are deliberately left out.

All rows were checked on **29 Oct 2026** unless marked otherwise. Each source code in the table links to its page; all codes are also listed in [Sources](#sources).
